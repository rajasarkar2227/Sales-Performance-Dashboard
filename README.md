# Sales-Performance-Dashboard
An interactive Sales Performance Dashboard built to transform sales transaction data into actionable business insights. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of revenue, orders, products, customers, regions, promotions, and returns.

## 🎯 Project Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze sales performance and provide business stakeholders with a clear, interactive way to:

* Monitor revenue and order performance
* Identify monthly sales trends
* Compare regional performance
* Understand new vs. returning customers
* Identify top revenue-contributing products
* Evaluate promotional performance
* Monitor returned revenue and orders
* Compare salesperson performance
* Identify areas requiring business attention

### 📈 Dashboard Analysis
1. **Revenue Trend** \
The monthly revenue trend helps identify periods of growth, decline, and potential seasonality.
The dashboard shows fluctuations throughout the year, with revenue reaching notable peaks in January, April, June, and October.
2. **Revenue by Region** \
Regional performance is compared across:
East
North
South
Central
West
The East region generates the highest revenue in the displayed dashboard.

3. **Customer Analysis** \
The dashboard compares:
New Customers
Returning Customers
This provides insight into customer acquisition versus retention and highlights opportunities to improve repeat purchases.

4. **Product Contribution** \
Products are ranked by their contribution to overall revenue.
Printer is the leading product contributor at approximately 18.71%,

5. **Promotion Analysis** \
The dashboard analyzes revenue associated with different promotion categories, including:
- FREESHIP
- WINTER15
- SAVE10
- Other / unspecified promotions
This can help evaluate which promotional strategies are associated with stronger sales.

6. **Returns Analysis** \
The 25.68% returned rate is one of the most important findings in the dashboard.
Returned revenue and returned orders are analyzed month by month to identify periods where returns may have a significant impact on business performance.

7. **Salesperson Performance** \
The salesperson table evaluates:
* Net Revenue
* Net Orders
* Average Order Value
* Returned Rate
* Returned Rate Growth

This allows management to identify both high-performing salespeople and unusual return-rate patterns.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
🔴 **High Return Rate** \
The overall return rate is 25.68%, which is significant enough to warrant further investigation.

Possible areas for investigation include: 

1. Product quality
2. Order fulfillment
3. Customer expectations
4. Shipping issues
5. Product-specific return patterns

🖨️ **Product Performance** \
Printer contributes approximately 18.71% of revenue, making it the strongest product category in the dashboard.

🌎 **Regional Performance** \
The East region records the highest revenue among the displayed regions, suggesting an opportunity to investigate what drives its stronger performance.

👥 **Customer Retention** \
New customers represent a substantially larger share than returning customers.
This suggests an opportunity to improve: \

* Customer retention
* Repeat purchases
* Loyalty programs
* Personalized marketing

🔄 **Return Management** \
Return performance varies across months and salespeople. Further analysis could determine whether returns are concentrated around specific:

* Products
* Regions
* Salespeople
* Promotion types
* Time periods
